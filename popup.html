Indeed Radius Filter

v1.12, Show on map button in job detail

Load all jobs on this page, then keep only the ones near you, anywhere in Canada.

Place

Distance method, better than town center

Best method: exact address from the job details, including postal code, then driving distance and time from OSRM, the free OpenStreetMap router. Indeed commute time on the card, for example 45-minute commute, is used first when it is there. Town center straight line is only a fallback.

Speed and rest

Default is 1 minute rest every 2 minutes of work. Rest only starts if loading takes longer than the work time. Short lists finish before any rest.

Options

Page by page: Load all loads only the page you are on. When you go to the Next or Previous page, the map updates automatically to that page. Automated: Load all loads this page, then goes to the next page by itself, loads it, and so on, up to 10 pages, and the map collects all of them. Click Stop to stop automated mode.

Map view: OpenStreetMap with your center, a radius circle, and a pin for each loaded job. Green is inside your radius, red is outside. Drag the blue center pin to move your center, change the radius on the map, then click Filter by circle or Filter by map view. Click a pin to see the job and open it.

Open an Indeed search page first, then use the buttons above.

Canada coverage: the extension has town coordinates for more than 100 Canadian cities, from Vancouver to St. John's. If a city is not in the list, it tries a free lookup for that city name in Canada, and it is cached. Jobs that only show a province or a vague place are hidden only if the West Island box above is checked.

Safety: Slow is the default. Fast is faster but easier for Indeed to flag. If you see a CAPTCHA or a verify you are human page, the extension stops by itself. Do not keep clicking Load all after that.
